Main Vision:
- Web based app to help Synagogue management to manage all its aspects

Details:
- A user can have multiple roles in a synagogue, while having different set of roles in another

- Onboarding:
- Anyone can create and manage a synagogue
- Guests don't need to sign-up

Management Roles:
- Receiving Donations
- Manage prayer times
- Manage the order of the Torah Aliyahs based on deceased relatives and other considerations
- Manage scrolls in a fair manner
- Send personal messages to other members
- Send community-level messages
- Publish Torah Lessons (repeating or one-times)
- Share a url to join the synagogue
- Share qr-code to join the synagogue
- Create a list of members (based on phone number) and send bulk invitation
- Seats arrangement (default + special occasions)

Members Features:
- Declare relative deceased
- Watch when their scroll will be used
- Watch the history of the times their scroll was read (+compare statistics with other scrolls)
- Watch praying times
- Watch messages
- Send requests and questions to the Gabay
- Pay donations and membership dues
- Define family links

Guests Features:
- Watch praying times
- Search for synagogues (based on location, praying times, etc.)
- Donate to synagogues

User types/Roles:
- Gabay
- Member
- Guest

UI:
- Multilingual:
  - Hebrew(default)/English/More in the future
  - Support both LTR and RTL languages
- Views:
  - Guest:
    - Daily times (sunrise, sunset, dawn, etc.). can modify location
    - "Find close minyan"
    - Donate
    - Send message to a synagogue's gabay
    - Browse synagogues
  - Member:
    - Switch between synagogues
    - Everything in guest
    - Customized dashboard
    - Upcoming lessons
    - Upcoming aliyot
    - Messages (can be a member of more than just a single synagogue)
    - Upcoming Yerzeits
  - Gabay:
    - Everything in member

- Scopes:
  - Donations
  - Aliyot board
  - Messages
  - Lesson publishing
  - Scroll management
  - Member directory
  - Seats arrangement
  - Finance exports
